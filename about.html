The Hope-Chain

Our four-pillar system ensures that every Satoshi you donate is converted into physical resources for those in need.

1. BTC Entry

Donations arrive in our secure multisig wallet, bypassing high-fee traditional banks.

2. Smart Audit

Funds are automatically allocated to specific field budgets: 80% Food, 20% Medicine.

3. Local Buy

We settle with local vendors using Lightning Network to ensure zero delays in food delivery.

4. Proof-of-Gift

Geotagged photos of the delivery are uploaded, completing the transparency loop.
