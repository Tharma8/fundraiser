The "Northern Hope" Methodology

1. Local Sourcing

We don't ship from overseas. We buy directly from Northern farmers within 50 miles of the need, boosting the local economy while providing fresh nutrition.

2. Blockchain Audit

Using the Bitcoin ledger, every cent is public. You can watch the funds move from your wallet to our food distribution centers in real-time.

3. GPS Verification

Our field teams use GPS-tagged mobile proof-of-delivery. When the food reaches a child, the location is logged and shared on our dashboard.
